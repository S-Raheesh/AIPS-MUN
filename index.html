Invitation to the Delegation

Office of the Secretary-General

Distinguished Faculty Advisors, Head Delegates, and Esteemed Participants,

On behalf of the Secretariat and the Executive Boards of AIPS MUN 2026, it is our distinct honor to invite delegations from our school and our six sister partner institutions to the upcoming edition of our parliamentary simulation on October 9 and 10, 2026.

The world stage demands more than rhetoric; it demands rigorous substantive synthesis, strategic negotiation, and the courage to find common ground amidst ideological gridlock. Whether addressing structural inequalities in ECOSOC, guarding global security in the UNSC, confronting acute rapid shifts in CCC, protecting fundamental rights in UNHRC, or chronicling the truth with the International Press, every delegate will be challenged to embody authentic diplomatic acumen.

Lakshmi Praba Secretary-General, AIPS MUN 2026
Raheesh Deputy Secretary-General, AIPS MUN 2026

Official Itinerary

08:00 - 09:00

Delegate Verification & Placard Distribution

Registration desks open at the main reception foyer. Delegation kit collection.

09:00 - 10:15

Opening Plenary & Gavel Ceremony

Keynote addresses from the Secretariat, introduction of the Executive Boards, and the opening gavel strike.

10:30 - 13:00

Committee Session I

Roll call, adoption of the agenda, and establishment of the General Speakers List (GSL).

13:00 - 14:00

Official Luncheon & Unmoderated Caucus

Informal networking and preliminary bloc negotiations across councils.

14:00 - 16:30

Committee Session II

Moderated debate on primary clauses, working paper consultations, and early crisis directives.

08:30 - 11:00

Committee Session III

Formal presentation of Draft Resolutions, amendment deliberations, and defense speeches.

11:15 - 13:00

Committee Session IV & Final Voting

Substantive voting procedure on resolutions, crisis freeze conclusions, and International Press conferences.

13:00 - 14:00

Luncheon & Photography

Council group portraits and informal interactions between delegations.

14:30 - 16:30

Closing Valedictory & Awards Ceremony

Secretariat address, awarding of gavels and citations, and formal adjournment sine die.
